01 / Start here

Observation details

Observe from a safe public area. Only answer what you can see or confirm from accessible project information. You do not need to visit daily.

Select a construction item to display its specific observation questions. Answer only the questions relevant to what you can safely see or verify.

For observation questions: Observed = you saw the condition described; Not observed = you looked but did not see that condition; Unable to see = the area or item was not visible; Not applicable = the question does not relate to this activity.

For Yes/No questions: select Yes or No based on what you can confirm; otherwise choose Unable to determine or Not applicable.

For document-comparison questions: select Matches / confirmed from documents, Difference or not confirmed, Documents not available, or Not applicable. Only select “confirmed” when you have the relevant approved document.

For written-answer questions: enter the location, activity, quantity or other requested details. Leave irrelevant questions unanswered. Observed does not mean technically compliant, and Not observed does not prove something is missing.
02 / Site observations

What did you see?

Answer only what you can safely observe. If a question needs a count or description, enter that information rather than choosing Yes or No.

Photos are embedded in exported HTML reports, not saved with text drafts. This offline form does not upload files to Drive.

03 / Verify building delivery

Building, classroom and storey verification

Record one building at a time. Use the current approved plans, including approved revisions. Leave unavailable figures blank. A visible room is not necessarily complete or formally accepted.

04 / Submit your observation

Reporting and follow-up

For immediate danger, contact the school or site safety officer. Do not enter restricted areas or attempt technical inspection.
